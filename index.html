Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Aurora has no account, no server and no analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

In Settings → Data, tap Back up data and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new phone, tap Restore from backup and pick that file. Your regular iPhone backup also includes the app's data.

How is the savings forecast worked out?

Your income, minus your bills and subscriptions, minus your everyday spending. For finished months Aurora uses what you logged; for this month and later it uses your Everyday budget (or what you've logged, if that's more). Tap the forecast card in the app to see every month.

Does Aurora convert currencies?

No. Pick your currency in Settings; amounts are shown in it as you enter them.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Aurora. For the Category widget, touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget to pick a category.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items in the app at any time, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
