SupportPrivacy Policy

The short version

Aurora is a personal expense tracker. It does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What Aurora stores, and where

  • Your entries: bills, subscriptions, everyday expenses, categories, budgets, income and bonuses. They are stored only on your device, inside the app.
  • Home Screen widgets: the app saves a small summary of the current month in a container shared only with its own widgets, so they can show your numbers.
  • Settings: currency, appearance, income, budgets and your savings goal are stored on your device.

Aurora has no account, no sign-in, no server and no analytics or advertising tools. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Backups and exports

  • Back up data and Export data (CSV) create files on your device. They only leave your iPhone if you choose where to save or send them (for example iCloud Drive, Files, Mail or AirDrop). Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups may include the app's data, as for any app. Apple handles those backups.

Crash reports

iOS may give Aurora reports about crashes or freezes (through Apple's MetricKit). Aurora keeps the last few on your device. They are never sent anywhere automatically; you can share them yourself from Settings → Share crash reports if you want to help fix a problem. They contain technical information about the app, not your financial entries.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. You can also delete individual items in the app at any time.

Children

Aurora is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
